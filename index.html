OUR STORY SINCE 1997

Renewing the world from people to planet

In 1997, we set out on a mission to support sustainable development in India. Initially, we lobbied for better energy efficiency; after successful policy interventions, we expanded our efforts into urban waste management.

At the time, urban landfills were overflowing with single-use plastic waste while nearby slum communities struggled in severe poverty and exploitation. Conserve stepped in by inventing patented technology to convert plastic bags into Handmade Recycled Plastic (HRP), training grassroot ragpickers into skilled artisans, and giving marginalized families dignified, sustainable livelihoods.

Partner With Conserve India →

Our Core Initiatives

  • Capacity Building: 7+ years training local artisans in stitching, weaving, block printing, and Fair Trade labor rights.
  • Grassroot Recyclers: 15+ years transforming waste pickers with 2 years of technical and business training.
  • UNHCR Refugee Empowerment: Supported Afghani refugee women in establishing their own production brand, Akseer.
  • Project Sahyog: Mobilized community kitchens and relief responders during COVID-19, ranked in India's top 50.
